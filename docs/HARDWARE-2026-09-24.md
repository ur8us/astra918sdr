# Linux receiver bring-up — 2026-09-24

Receiver `C1E27EA41B7ECCC3`, RP2350 revision A2, QFN60 (RP2350A),
4096 KiB flash. Picoprobe `2e8a:000c:E46214B0A7101C28`, SWD 1000 kHz.
An HF antenna is connected. No frequency generator or transmitter was used.

## Backup and programming

Both Cortex-M33 cores were discovered over SWD. The previous CMX918 firmware
(`c0de:0918`) accepted its BOOTSEL command. `picotool info -a` confirmed chip
identity and flash capacity; `picotool save -a -v` backed up and verified all
4 MiB before programming. Backup SHA-256:
`cbbc2f055463e6e8be635de7e2ce802e9537b53a250b42fa3a4b717e47d293dc`.
The backup is ignored at `artifacts/hardware-20260924/original-flash.bin`.

`probe-rs download --verify` programmed the RP2350A ELF, followed by reset.
Astra enumerated as `c0de:091a`: audio interfaces 0/1 bound to snd-usb-audio,
CAT 2/3 to cdc_acm, vendor 4 without a kernel driver. CMX918 I2C initialization
and wide FIR upload succeeded. ALSA recognizes asynchronous mono S16_LE at
12 kHz, maximum 26-byte packets.

## DSP failure and correction

The initial image fell behind the 240 ksps DMA capture stream. A 512-output
sample block has a 4267 us budget; measured polling intervals reached 7438 us,
and the bounded ring correctly stopped capture on overrun. Borrowing filter
coefficients instead of copying arrays reduced that interval to 5550 us but
was still insufficient.

The corrected convolution uses mirrored history buffers and symmetric tap
pairs (conjugate symmetry for the SSB filter). This halves multiplications and
removes per-tap ring wrapping while preserving the filter lengths and design.
Core 1 has a 64 KiB stack for the larger filter state. RTT reports aggregate
block processing time every 1024 blocks.

An initial 30-second run measured 2938–2940 us mean per block, with a maximum
3776 us during startup, and no capture fault. These are initial measurements,
not a completed simultaneous I/Q/audio endurance qualification.

All 53 host tests and seven cross-process acceptance tests pass. A new broadband
test compares paired convolution with a full-convolution oracle in both
sidebands at −45/0/+45 kHz; results differ by at most one PCM count. External
WSJT-X decoders still decode FT8, FT4 and WSPR after the production DSP in
USB/+45 kHz and LSB/−45 kHz software tests.

## Physical audio and CAT

Direct CDC queries returned `ID020;`, the applied dial and ready status.
CAT successfully tuned 14.074 MHz. Ten seconds of ALSA recording produced
120000 audio samples with nonzero signal; diagnostic counters remained zero.

An isolated WSJT-X 2.7.0 profile uses the physical PipeWire receiver source
and physical CDC port. Its 48 kHz capture request is resampled from UAC's
12 kHz stream. On-air FT8 decoded `CQ EA4GA IN80` at 07:49:45 UTC on 14.074 MHz.
Transmit and uploads remain disabled. Normal profiles and default audio routes
were not changed. Evidence is under ignored `artifacts/hardware-wsjtx-*` and
`artifacts/hardware-20260924/`.

Selecting FT4 in WSJT-X retuned the physical receiver from 14.074 to 14.080 MHz
via CAT, confirmed by the application's polled dial status. Its band dropdown
then selected 7.0475 MHz. No FT4 decode appeared during the approximately
four-minute attempt across these two bands; this is inconclusive on-air
evidence. After the combined FT8/FT4 session, `ZZST` still reported ready with
zero capture faults, audio underruns, overruns or stalls.

A subsequent ten-minute WSPR attempt included four complete receive intervals
at 14.0956 MHz. `ALL_WSPR.TXT` remained empty: no on-air WSPR decode, also an
inconclusive result. Final CAT status again reported ready and all four fault
counters zero. No sustained I/Q stream was running during these receive tests.

## Remaining checks

Vendor-interface access currently needs the supplied udev rule installed by
an administrator: the host client returns `Access denied (insufficient
permissions)`, and noninteractive sudo requires a password. Sustained
simultaneous I/Q/audio, SDR++/GUI hardware controls, I/Q stall recovery, explicit
Save and interrupted-save recovery are not yet qualified. Native Windows/macOS
USB testing and physical power cycling have not run. Generator testing and
calibrated RF measurements are excluded entirely.

The receive-only helpers are `scripts/test_hardware.py` (explicit serial,
audio/CAT/IQ and optional reversible control/stall checks) and
`scripts/live_hardware_wsjtx.py` (isolated physical WSJT-X profile). Neither
helper flashes firmware or saves receiver settings.

After installing `packaging/70-astra918.rules` and reloading/retriggering udev,
close WSJT-X and other receiver controllers before running the direct test:

```sh
python3 scripts/test_hardware.py --serial C1E27EA41B7ECCC3 \
  --seconds 30 --controls --stall --output artifacts/hardware-controls
python3 scripts/test_hardware.py --serial C1E27EA41B7ECCC3 \
  --seconds 1800 --output artifacts/hardware-endurance
```

The direct test measures hardware transport independently of the applications;
it does not replace the separate simultaneous WSJT-X/SDR++ application test.
It records raw audio, per-second state and summary JSON in the chosen folder.
These full vendor-dependent helper runs remain unexecuted in this record.
